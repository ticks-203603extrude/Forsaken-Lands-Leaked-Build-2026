## 🕹️ About

Forsaken Lands is a fantasy roguelite deckbuilder where a squad of heroes explores ancient ruins, battles strange creatures, and challenges the gods. Build distinct decks, uncover tangled stories, or enter procedurally generated Dream runs with solo or up to three-player co-op. This is a raw leaked pre-release build that surfaced before the finished release.

For players who want strategic fantasy deckbuilding, replayable roguelite runs, and rough early-build energy in solo or co-op play.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🃏 **Hero Deckbuilding** — Lead distinct heroes with personal cards, abilities, playstyles, and progression paths.
- ⚔️ **Turn-Based Battles** — Fight creatures, mini-bosses, and powerful bosses through strategic card combat.
- 🌲 **Ancient Ruins** — Explore shadowy forests, forgotten locations, strange events, and a world built on myth.
- 📖 **Story Mode** — Follow character-focused campaigns filled with backstories, relationships, and intertwined destinies.
- 🌀 **Dream Mode** — Enter procedurally generated adventures with changing modifiers, events, and enemies.
- 🤝 **Three-Player Co-op** — Team up online, coordinate tactics, and progress your heroes together.
- 🛕 **Challenge the Gods** — Push beyond the ruins and uncover the forces shaping the Forsaken Lands.
